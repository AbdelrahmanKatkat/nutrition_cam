      What does “serving” mean?

      A serving is one portion of the finished dish: the whole recipe divided by the number of servings. Every nutrition value on this page is for one serving.

      Set the number to how many portions you will actually split the dish into. A bigger number means smaller portions, so the calories, protein and vitamins per serving go down; a smaller number means bigger portions. Nothing is re-calculated by the AI, it just divides.
