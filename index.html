Help & Settings

Meal photo: tick NutriAI, add a photo, press Scan Now.

Packaged food: photograph the barcode (or type its digits), tick Open Food Facts, press Scan Now.

Tap the refresh icon next to Camera / Upload to start a new item. Until you do, your photo stays loaded: edit the description and press Scan Now again to re-run.

NutriChat: after a scan, ask it for healthier swaps, higher-protein versions or a different portion size.

The AI key is held by a secure server, never in this page. Requests are limited to protect the quota; if you hit the limit, wait for the countdown.
